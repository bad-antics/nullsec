USAGE WORKFLOW

Step 1: Launch NULLSEC
  ./nullsec-launcher.py

Step 2: Enter Execute Console
  Press [E] from main menu

Step 3: Launch Shodan Browser
  nullsec@exec > shodan

Step 4: Search and Gather Intelligence
  shodan@browser > search apache country:US
  shodan@browser > host 203.0.113.50
  shodan@browser > export 203.0.113.50
  shodan@browser > scan 203.0.113.50
  shodan@browser > save apache_servers.json

Step 5: Exit and Exploit
  shodan@browser > exit
  nullsec@exec > exec nmap -sV -sC 203.0.113.50

SEARCH CAPABILITIES
