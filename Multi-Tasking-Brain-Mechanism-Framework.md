## **THE CORE PROTOCOLS TRANSLATED**

### **1. Pre-Work Trinity Activation (5 min)**
**Before ANY focused work:**
- **Accountability:** Text/tell someone your specific goal and timeline
- **Visual Focus:** Pick one spot, stare 30-60 seconds (soft gaze, don't blink excessively)
- **Urgency:** Set a timer, create mild stakes ("If I don't finish, I redo this tomorrow")

**→ This triggers epinephrine + acetylcholine from brainstem & nucleus basalis**
**→ Your brain NOW HAS TO CHANGE. It's chemically mandatory.**

### **2. Visual Anchoring During Work**
- **Primary task:** Narrow visual cone (focus on one screen area)
- **Secondary tasks:** Peripheral awareness (don't lose the big picture)
- **When distracted:** Return gaze to anchor point (this re-engages nucleus basalis ACh)

### **3. Post-Learning NSDR (10-20 min)**
**Immediately after focused work:**
- Close eyes
- Body scan or yoga nidra (YouTube has 10-20 min guides)
- OR: Just breathe, no input, let mind wander
- **DO NOT check phone, email, or start new task**

**→ This is when neuroplasticity actually happens**
**→ The circuits you highlighted during work get strengthened**
**→ Learning rate accelerates 2-3x**

### **4. Sleep Optimization**
- **Protect deep sleep:** No screens 2 hours before bed
- **Cool, dark room:** 65-68°F ideal
- **90-min cycles:** Wake between cycles, not during
- **What you practice today gets encoded tonight**

### **5. Curiosity as a Chemical Trigger**
- **Ask questions BEFORE you know answers**
- **"What if..." thinking releases ACh**
- **Listener sponge mindset = nucleus basalis primed**
